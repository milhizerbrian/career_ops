## Resume Writing Rules

Use these as hard constraints when generating any resume or cover letter.

- Frame Brian as an operator, not a relationship manager. Operator means builds systems, aligns stakeholders, drives execution, closes gaps, and owns commercial outcomes.
- Do not frame him as someone who merely manages relationships, provides support, serves as a point of contact, or facilitates communication.
- Every bullet should show at least two of: measurable impact, scope, method or system, stakeholder, and business outcome.
- Strong bullets show the problem solved, the decision or method Brian used, and the measurable result.
- Do not invent metrics, titles, employers, tools, certifications, dates, or education. If a needed metric is missing, use [X%] or [$XM ARR].
- Never use: results-driven, proven track record, passionate about, dynamic, thought leader, world-class, best-in-class, synergy, leverage as jargon, empowered.
- No em dashes. No "Responsible for..." phrasing. No three consecutive gerund-opening bullets. No consecutive bullets starting with the same verb.
- Include at least one specific platform, stakeholder type, or use case per role when space allows so domain knowledge is visible.
- Before finalizing, the top half of page one must show seniority, ARR or portfolio scale, retention or expansion result, and cybersecurity domain credibility.
